ACME CORPORATION
Q4 and Full-Year 2023 Earnings Conference Call
February 6, 2024

OPERATOR: Good morning and welcome to Acme Corporation's fourth-quarter and
full-year 2023 earnings conference call. Joining us today are Chief Executive
Officer Maria Chen and Chief Financial Officer David Okafor.

MARIA CHEN (CEO): Thank you, and good morning everyone. 2023 was a record year for
Acme. We delivered full-year revenue of $4.2 billion, up 12% year over year, and
expanded gross margin to 41.2%.

Let me highlight our key growth drivers. First, our Cloud Services segment grew
28% for the year and now represents 35% of total revenue. Second, our
international expansion continued to accelerate, with the Asia-Pacific region
growing 24% as we opened new data-center capacity in Singapore and Tokyo. Third,
the launch of our AcmeAI automation platform drove a 15% increase in average
contract value among enterprise customers.

DAVID OKAFOR (CFO): Thank you, Maria. Turning to guidance. For fiscal year 2024,
we expect total revenue in the range of $4.7 billion to $4.9 billion, representing
growth of approximately 12% to 17%. We expect full-year diluted earnings per share
in the range of $5.10 to $5.30. We anticipate gross margin to remain in the range
of 41% to 42%, and we plan capital expenditures of approximately $350 million,
primarily for data-center expansion.

ANALYST (Goldman Sachs): Can you talk about the demand environment for Cloud
Services heading into 2024?

MARIA CHEN: Demand remains robust. Our backlog grew 31% year over year, and we are
seeing strong renewal rates above 95%. We are particularly encouraged by adoption
of AcmeAI, which we expect to be a meaningful contributor to 2024 growth.

ANALYST (Morgan Stanley): How should we think about margins given the capex
ramp?

DAVID OKAFOR: While the data-center investments create some near-term
depreciation headwind, we expect operating leverage in our software business to
more than offset it, keeping operating margin roughly flat to slightly up in 2024.

OPERATOR: That concludes today's call. Thank you for joining.
