TERM LOAN AGREEMENT

This Term Loan Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated as of April 15, 2024, by and
between SUMMIT MANUFACTURING LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the
"Borrower"), and FIRST MERIDIAN BANK, N.A., as administrative agent and lender
(the "Lender").

ARTICLE 1 — THE LOAN
Section 1.1 Commitment. The Lender agrees to make a term loan to the Borrower in
the aggregate principal amount of $25,000,000 (the "Loan"), advanced in a single
drawing on the Closing Date.

Section 1.2 Interest Rate. The outstanding principal balance of the Loan shall
bear interest at a rate per annum equal to Term SOFR plus an applicable margin of
3.25%. Interest shall be payable quarterly in arrears. Upon the occurrence and
during the continuance of an Event of Default, the applicable margin shall
increase by 2.00% (the "Default Rate").

Section 1.3 Maturity. The Loan shall mature and all outstanding principal,
together with accrued interest, shall be due and payable in full on the fifth
anniversary of the Closing Date (the "Maturity Date").

Section 1.4 Amortization. The principal of the Loan shall amortize in equal
quarterly installments of 1.25% of the original principal amount, with the
remaining balance due as a balloon payment on the Maturity Date.

Section 1.5 Prepayment. The Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole or in part at any
time without premium or penalty, upon three business days' prior written notice.

ARTICLE 6 — FINANCIAL COVENANTS
Section 6.1 Leverage Ratio. The Borrower shall not permit the ratio of
Consolidated Total Debt to Consolidated EBITDA, measured as of the last day of any
fiscal quarter, to exceed 3.50 to 1.00.

Section 6.2 Interest Coverage Ratio. The Borrower shall maintain a ratio of
Consolidated EBITDA to Consolidated Interest Expense of not less than 2.00 to 1.00,
tested quarterly.

Section 6.3 Minimum Liquidity. The Borrower shall maintain unrestricted cash and
cash equivalents of not less than $2,000,000 at all times.

Section 6.4 Limitation on Indebtedness. The Borrower shall not incur additional
indebtedness for borrowed money in excess of $1,000,000 in the aggregate without
the prior written consent of the Lender.

ARTICLE 7 — EVENTS OF DEFAULT
Section 7.1 Events of Default. Each of the following constitutes an "Event of
Default" under this Agreement:
(a) Payment Default. The Borrower fails to pay any principal when due, or any
    interest within five (5) business days after the due date.
(b) Covenant Default. The Borrower fails to comply with any financial covenant set
    forth in Article 6.
(c) Cross-Default. Any other indebtedness of the Borrower in excess of $500,000 is
    accelerated prior to its stated maturity.
(d) Insolvency. The Borrower commences a voluntary bankruptcy proceeding or has an
    involuntary proceeding commenced against it that is not dismissed within 60
    days.
(e) Misrepresentation. Any representation or warranty made by the Borrower proves
    to have been materially incorrect when made.

Section 7.2 Remedies. Upon an Event of Default, the Lender may declare the entire
unpaid principal balance and all accrued interest immediately due and payable and
may exercise all rights available under applicable law.
