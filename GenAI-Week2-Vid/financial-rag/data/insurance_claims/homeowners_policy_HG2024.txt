SECTION I — COVERAGES
Coverage A — Dwelling: $400,000. We cover the dwelling on the residence premises
shown in the Declarations, including structures attached to the dwelling.

Coverage B — Other Structures: $40,000. We cover other structures on the residence
premises set apart from the dwelling, such as a detached garage or shed.

Coverage C — Personal Property: $200,000. We cover personal property owned or used
by an insured while it is anywhere in the world.

Coverage D — Loss of Use: $80,000. If a covered loss makes the residence
uninhabitable, we cover the necessary increase in living expenses incurred by the
insured.

Coverage E — Personal Liability: $300,000 per occurrence. We pay for damages an
insured becomes legally obligated to pay due to bodily injury or property damage.

Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others: $5,000 per person.

DEDUCTIBLE
A deductible of $1,500 applies to each covered loss under Section I before we pay.
A separate wind/hail deductible of 2% of the Coverage A limit applies in
designated counties.

SECTION I — EXCLUSIONS
We do not insure for loss caused directly or indirectly by any of the following:
1. Flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of water,
   whether or not driven by wind.
2. Earthquake, land shock waves, or tremors before, during, or after a volcanic
   eruption, landslide, or earth movement.
3. War, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, or
   revolution.
4. Intentional loss, meaning any loss arising out of an act committed by or at the
   direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss.
5. Neglect, meaning the insured's failure to use all reasonable means to save and
   preserve property at and after the time of a loss.
6. Wear and tear, marring, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, rust, mold, or
   wet or dry rot.

SECTION I — CONDITIONS (CLAIM HANDLING)
Your Duties After Loss. In case of a loss, the insured must: (a) give prompt
notice to us or our agent; (b) protect the property from further damage; (c)
prepare an inventory of damaged personal property; and (d) submit a signed, sworn
proof of loss within 60 days of our request.

Loss Settlement. Covered losses to the dwelling are settled at replacement cost
without deduction for depreciation, provided repairs are completed. Personal
property is settled at actual cash value unless replacement-cost coverage is
endorsed.

Appraisal. If the insured and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either party
may demand an appraisal, in which each selects a competent appraiser and the two
appraisers select an umpire.
