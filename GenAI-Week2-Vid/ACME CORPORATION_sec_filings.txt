ACME CORPORATION
FORM 10-K
ANNUAL REPORT PURSUANT TO SECTION 13 OR 15(d) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934
For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023

PART I

ITEM 1. BUSINESS
Acme Corporation ("Acme," "we," or "the Company") is a diversified technology and
industrial products company headquartered in Wilmington, Delaware. The Company
operates through three reportable segments: Cloud Services, Industrial Automation,
and Consumer Products. Acme employed approximately 18,400 people as of
December 31, 2023, and sells its products and services in more than 40 countries.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS
Investing in our securities involves risk. The major risk factors identified by
management are summarized below.

Supply Chain Disruptions. We depend on a limited number of third-party suppliers
for critical components. Disruptions, including those caused by geopolitical
tensions, natural disasters, or pandemics, could materially impair our ability to
manufacture and deliver products on schedule.

Cybersecurity Threats. We store sensitive customer and proprietary data. A
significant breach of our information systems could result in regulatory
penalties, reputational harm, and loss of customer confidence.

Regulatory and Compliance Risk. We are subject to evolving data-privacy, export-
control, and environmental regulations across multiple jurisdictions. Changes in
these regulations could increase our costs of compliance or restrict our
operations.

Foreign Currency Risk. Approximately 38% of our revenue is denominated in
currencies other than the U.S. dollar, exposing us to exchange-rate volatility.

Competition. The markets we serve are highly competitive and subject to rapid
technological change. Several competitors have greater financial resources than
we do.

ITEM 3. LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
In March 2023, Beta Industries Inc. filed a patent-infringement lawsuit against
the Company in the U.S. District Court for the District of Delaware, seeking
damages of approximately $50 million. The Company believes the claims are without
merit and intends to defend the matter vigorously. Separately, the Company is
engaged in environmental remediation at a legacy manufacturing site in Ohio, for
which it has recorded a reserve of $7.5 million.

PART II

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
Results of Operations. Total revenue for fiscal year 2023 was $4.2 billion, an
increase of 12% compared to $3.75 billion in fiscal year 2022. The growth was
driven primarily by strong demand in our Cloud Services segment. Net income for
2023 was $480 million, or $5.05 per diluted share, compared to $412 million, or
$4.32 per diluted share, in 2022. Gross margin expanded to 41.2% from 39.8% in the
prior year, reflecting an improved product mix and operating efficiencies.

Liquidity and Capital Resources. As of December 31, 2023, the Company held cash
and cash equivalents of $1.1 billion and had total long-term debt of $900 million.
The Board declared a quarterly dividend of $0.32 per share.
